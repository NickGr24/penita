{% trans "What is Penița Dreptului?" %}

{% blocktrans %}Penița Dreptului is a professional legal portal dedicated to criminal procedural law and forensics from the Republic of Moldova. It offers scientific articles and specialty books written by Tudor Osoianu and Dinu Ostavciuc.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "How can I access the books and articles?" %}

{% blocktrans %}Articles are freely accessible to all visitors. Books may be free or paid; for paid books an account and a secure payment via the MAIB system are required.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "What legal topics does the portal cover?" %} {% url 'articles' as articles_url %}

{% blocktrans %}The portal covers three main directions: criminal procedural law, forensics and other related legal sciences, with a focus on the legislation and practice of the Republic of Moldova.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Who are the authors of the materials?" %} {% url 'osoianu' as osoianu_url %}{% url 'ostavciuc' as ostavciuc_url %}

{% blocktrans %}The materials are written by Tudor Osoianu, expert in criminal procedural law, and Dinu Ostavciuc, doctor habilitat in law, both with over 20 years of academic and practical experience.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "How is payment for the books made?" %}

{% blocktrans %}Payment is made online through the secure MAIB system with Visa and Mastercard cards. After payment confirmation you get permanent access to the purchased book in your personal account.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "How can I read the purchased books?" %}

{% blocktrans %}Books are read directly online in the portal's integrated viewer. You have permanent access to the content from your account, anytime and from any internet-connected device.{% endblocktrans %}
